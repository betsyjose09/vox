# Knotopian Institute of Technology (KIT) - Academic Handbook & Policies

## Attendance Regulations
1. Minimum Attendance Requirement: All registered undergraduate students must maintain a minimum overall attendance of 75% in each enrolled subject to be eligible to sit for end-semester university examinations.
2. Condonation Range: Students with attendance between 65% and 74.9% may apply for medical condonation subject to submission of certified medical records and approval by the Dean of Academics with payment of a condonation fee.
3. Debarment: Any student securing below 65% attendance is strictly debarred from taking end-semester exams in that course and must re-register for the course in subsequent semesters.

## Leave and On-Duty (OD) Regulations
1. Leave Submission: Leave applications must be submitted in advance through the KIT portal or voice assistant.
2. Examination Conflict Policy: No personal leave or On-Duty (OD) is granted on dates marked as Midterm or End-Semester Examinations on the official college academic calendar. Any such leave request is strictly rejected by policy.
3. On-Duty Permissibility: On-Duty (OD) is permissible for up to 10 working days per academic semester for approved external events such as national hackathons, technical symposiums, research paper presentations, and inter-collegiate sports meets.

## Evaluation and Grading System
1. Internal Assessments: Two continuous assessment tests (CAT-1 and CAT-2) constitute 40% of the internal score, with assignments and lab performance comprising 10%.
2. End-Semester Examination: Carries 50% weightage.
3. Pass Criterion: A student must obtain at least 45% in the end-semester exam and 50% overall to pass.
